# SOPPilot AI Case Study

## Overview

SOPPilot AI is an AI-assisted SOP, checklist, and training document generator for small-business teams. It helps managers turn rough process notes into standard operating procedures, checklists, training plans, quality control guides, implementation plans, and downloadable SOP packages.

## Problem

Small and mid-sized businesses often operate from tribal knowledge. Processes may live in a manager’s head, a scattered note, a chat message, or a quick verbal explanation.

This creates common problems:

- Team members perform the same process differently
- New hires receive inconsistent training
- Managers repeatedly explain the same process
- Important steps get missed
- Handoffs are unclear
- Quality control is inconsistent
- Escalation paths are not documented
- Process improvement is difficult because the process is not clearly written down

## Solution

SOPPilot AI creates a structured workflow for process documentation.

A user enters process-safe information, including:

- Process name
- Department
- Process owner role
- Process frequency
- Risk level
- Team size
- Trigger event
- Process goal
- Inputs needed
- Tools used
- Rough steps
- Quality standard
- Escalation path

The app then generates practical process documentation a manager can immediately use.

## Business Impact

SOPPilot AI demonstrates how lightweight AI-assisted workflows can help businesses improve process consistency without requiring enterprise documentation software.

Potential benefits include:

- Better training consistency
- Clearer process ownership
- Better handoffs
- Better quality control

- Stronger accountability
